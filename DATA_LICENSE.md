# Demo data and release scope

The original fictional investor profiles in `profiles/`, startup scenarios in
`startups/`, and illustrative material in `EXAMPLES.md` and its generated `docs/examples.html` and `templates/` are
provided under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE), to the extent copyright
or similar rights are held by the project contributors. The code, documentation
and project assets are also distributed under that license, except the separately
licensed included research pack and its notices under `packs/`.

These are invented software examples, not renamed or anonymized real profiles.
Their names, preferences, dates, quantities and scenarios do not establish any
real investment mandate, offer, historical event, survival outcome or endorsement.
Any resemblance to an actual organization is unintended. Illustrative metrics
are not benchmarks or targets. Unknown fields remain unknown.

The schema retains `retrieved_date` and `published_date` for compatibility. In
fictional records these identify the scenario authoring date; they do not claim
that external research was retrieved or independently published. Source links
point to the included scenario specifications, not fabricated external sources.

No license or permission is granted for third-party articles, scraped material,
real-world research records, names, trademarks, or user-supplied data. No ownership
is claimed over facts or material outside the contributors' rights. A project
license cannot supply rights held by others.

Optional research Guide Packs imported locally retain their publisher's terms.
Importing a package does not apply this project's MIT license to it. Imports are
not included in public builds or exports. See [GUIDE_PACKS.md](GUIDE_PACKS.md)
for the package format, preview, switching and local storage behavior.

The included reviewed package at `packs/guide-pack.json` is a convenience copy maintained
in the separate research repository. It retains its [editorial licence](packs/LICENSE.md),
[source bases](packs/SOURCES.md) and [third-party notices](packs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
It is embedded in the app as the default for new browser workspaces, separately from
the optional fictional demo. Existing saved library choices are preserved. No private archives or raw source material are included.
Merely adding attribution or marking a record reviewed does not establish permission.

Build checks validate the included research package and enforce fictional metadata,
local provenance links and indexed files for the separate demo. They check release composition; they cannot establish legal rights or
detect every instance of copied content. Contributions must be original or
accompanied by a documented compatible reuse basis.
