These are invented software examples, not renamed or anonymized real profiles. Their names, preferences, dates, quantities and scenarios do not establish any real investment mandate, offer, historical event, survival outcome or endorsement. Any resemblance to an actual organization is unintended. Illustrative metrics are not benchmarks or targets. Unknown fields remain unknown.

The schema retains retrieved_date and published_date for compatibility. In fictional records these identify the scenario authoring date; they do not claim that external research was retrieved or independently published. Source links point to the included scenario specifications, not fabricated external sources.

Optional research Guide Packs imported locally retain their publisher's terms. Importing a package does not apply this project's MIT license to it. Imports are not included in public builds or exports. See GUIDE_PACKS.md for the package format, preview, switching and local storage behavior.

The included reviewed package at packs/guide-pack.json is a convenience copy maintained in the separate research repository. It retains its editorial licence, source bases and third-party notices. It is embedded in the app as the default for new browser workspaces, separately from the optional fictional demo. Existing saved library choices are preserved. No private archives or raw source material are included. Merely adding attribution or marking a record reviewed does not establish permission.

Build checks validate the included research package and enforce fictional metadata, local provenance links and indexed files for the separate demo. They check release composition; they cannot establish legal rights or detect every instance of copied content. Contributions must be original or accompanied by a documented compatible reuse basis.
